Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your MyStockMate account without reinstalling the app.

Developer: Tumelo Moreo

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email to tumelo@moreoporjects.co.za.
  2. Use the email address associated with your MyStockMate account.
  3. Use the subject MyStockMate Account Deletion Request.
  4. State that you want your account and associated data deleted. Do not send your password.

Email an account deletion request

What data is covered

A deletion request covers your MyStockMate account and associated profile information, inventory records and uploaded product images. Information that must be retained for legal, security or dispute-resolution purposes may be kept for as long as necessary for that purpose.

Verification and processing

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request. We will provide confirmation when the request has been completed. Do not include your password or other login credentials in your request.
