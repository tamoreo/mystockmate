Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyStockMate handles information when you use the MyStockMate mobile application.

1. Developer and contact

MyStockMate is developed and operated by Tumelo Moreo. For privacy questions or requests, contact tumelo@moreoporjects.co.za.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, MyStockMate may process the following information:

  • Account information: first name, surname, business name, business type, email address and contact number.
  • Authentication information: information needed to create and secure your account. Your password is handled by our authentication provider; MyStockMate does not need to store your password in its own profile records.
  • Inventory information: item names, item types, quantities, sizes, descriptions and prices that you enter.
  • Product images: images you choose to upload for your inventory items.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into inventory fields unless it is necessary for your own business use.

3. How we use information

We use this information to create and manage your account, authenticate you, display and manage your inventory, store product images, provide app functionality, respond to support requests and handle account-deletion requests.

4. Service providers and storage

MyStockMate uses Supabase for account authentication, database services and storage of uploaded product images. Information is processed by Supabase to provide these services and is subject to the provider's terms and privacy practices. We do not publish your inventory as a public catalogue.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect the app, its users or our legal rights.

5. Data security

We use service-provider security features and reasonable technical measures intended to protect account and inventory information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your login credentials private and notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain account and inventory information while it is needed to provide the service or meet legitimate legal and security requirements. You can request deletion of your MyStockMate account and associated data using the instructions on our Account Deletion page, or by emailing tumelo@moreoporjects.co.za.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes. If any data is retained, it will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose.

7. Children's privacy

MyStockMate is intended for business inventory management and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
